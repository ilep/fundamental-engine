# Accounting-policy checklist

Before accepting an analysis, record decisions for:

- goodwill and acquired intangibles;
- operating and finance leases;
- excess cash and non-operating investments;
- stock-based compensation and dilution;
- restructuring, impairments, and acquisition costs;
- capitalized R&D and other internally generated assets;
- maintenance versus growth capex;
- cash, effective, or normalized tax rate;
- acquisitions and disposals affecting incremental ROIC;
- currency and reporting-unit consistency.

The engine exposes these decisions as assumptions and warnings. It does not attempt to hide
them behind a single apparently precise figure.
